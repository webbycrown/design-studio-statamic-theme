We stopped treating the hero as a place to park a mood film. The first screen now has a single claim, one supporting line, and a path forward. Motion, if we use it, has to earn the extra load.

On mobile that means the headline stays inside the first viewport and the button is not buried under a fold of texture. We prototype on a phone before we polish the desktop art direction.

A recent launch used a still photograph instead of a looping reel. Load time fell, and the same image became the campaign print. One strong frame beat thirty seconds of ambient motion.
