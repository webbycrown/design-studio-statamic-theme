A food brand wanted a coined word. Every option needed a pronunciation key. We went back to language they already used with customers and found a short name hiding in their own menu notes.

Good names are easy to search, easy to say, and hard to confuse with a neighbour. Trademark is a later gate. Speech is the first one.

The identity grew quickly after that because the word already had a tone. We did not have to invent a personality to paper over a brittle name.
