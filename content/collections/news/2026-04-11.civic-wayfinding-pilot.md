We are piloting a wayfinding set for a waterfront path: arrival, decision, and confirmation. The type is high contrast and the arrows are almost boring. That is the compliment we want.

Colour codes three routes. Icons stay secondary because icons go out of date when a cafe changes name. Words last longer in civic space.

If the pilot holds through the summer crowds, the full street set follows in autumn. We will share the before-and-after counts when the city publishes them.
