A publisher asked for a house face with more bite. We set the same article in four families and printed them. The loudest option fell apart in footnotes. The quietest one held at every size.

We still use a display cut for covers. The text face does the daily work. Mixing those roles is how a library of books stays one brand.

Licensing mattered too. A face we cannot use on the site and the jacket is a half solution. The family we chose covers both without a second invoice.
