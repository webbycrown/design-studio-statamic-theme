North Course trains people who are already tired at the end of a workday. We designed large type, short lessons, and a progress bar that tells the truth.

Illustration is used for a concept, not as wallpaper. Certificates are plain enough to print and pin.

The organisation can add a course without a designer. We built the templates so the next year of content does not need a rebrand.
