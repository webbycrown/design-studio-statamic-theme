We ran an internal study: how far can a brand go with three colours? Far enough for a year of campaigns, if the photography is directed and the type has contrast.

The signal colour is used like punctuation. It marks a price, a date, or a sold-out state. It does not wash entire pages.

Two clients adopted the rule this spring. Their social feeds look more expensive because they finally match. Consistency reads as quality even when the budget is ordinary.
