We ran a compressed rebrand for a regional gallery. Monday was language. Tuesday was marks. Wednesday was colour and type. Thursday was applications. Friday was the system handoff.

The useful constraint was the wall. Every rejected direction stayed visible so we did not circle back. The winning mark was the third one we drew, not the tenth. It held on a ticket, a tote, and a two-storey banner.

We still write a two-page rationale after these weeks. Future teams need the why, not just the files. A fast week without notes becomes another rebrand in eighteen months.
