Looping textures used to be our default for energy. They also hid weak structure. This year we use motion to show state: a menu opening, a product rotating, a number counting to a real figure.

Reduced motion preferences are not optional. Every sequence has a still fallback. That discipline made the work clearer for everyone, not only for people who turn animation off.

A recent product page uses a 12-frame turntable instead of a 40-second film. Buyers can scrub. The studio can update a single angle without a reshoot. Helpful motion is also maintainable motion.
