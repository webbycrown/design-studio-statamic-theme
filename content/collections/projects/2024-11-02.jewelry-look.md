Sieve makes small runs of jewellery. We directed a still-life set: daylight, a worn table, hands when needed, never a lifestyle couple.

The site is almost a catalogue. Materials and dimensions sit next to the price. Stories live in a journal, not on every product card.

Packaging is recycled board and a stamp. The unboxing matches the photographs, which is rarer than it should be.
