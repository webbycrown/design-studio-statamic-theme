Trust is visual before it is verbal. A cramped form, a heading that crashes into a photo, or a price that sits in a different typeface than the rest of the page all say the same thing: nobody is in charge.

Our reviews now start with spacing. We check the rhythm from the nav to the footer before we argue about a colour. When the grid is even, the brand feels looked after.

A finance client asked for more warmth. We did not add illustrations first. We opened the line height, warmed the paper tone, and let their existing photography sit larger. The site felt human without becoming casual.
