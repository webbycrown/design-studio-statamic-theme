We used to send a drive link and hope. Now every project ends with a numbered list: masters, exports, fonts, photo credits, voice notes, and who updates what.

The do-not list is the page clients thank us for later. It stops a well-meaning intern from stretching the mark or inventing a fourth green.

If you are a new client, ask to see a sample handoff. It tells you more about a studio than the homepage reel.
