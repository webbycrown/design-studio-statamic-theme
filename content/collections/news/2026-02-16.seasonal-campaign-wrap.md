We art-directed a spring campaign for a homewares brand. The sentence was eight words. Everything else was crop, paper, and a table shot in late light.

Eight formats from one idea is our favourite kind of production. It is cheaper, clearer, and easier to remember. Fragmented campaigns feel busy and then disappear.

The landing page used the poster crop, not a new lifestyle set. Shoppers recognised the work from the street. That recognition is the campaign doing its job.
