Identity work used to stop at the mark. Then the first campaign arrived with stock that fought the type. We now specify light, crop, and what the subject should be doing.

The guide is simple: three approved references, two forbidden habits, and a note on skin, food, or product shine. Photographers like clear edges. So do we.

A hotel group used the same guide across four properties and the feed finally looks like one place. That consistency is the brand working when we are not in the room.
