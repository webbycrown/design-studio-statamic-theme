Our previous site was a portfolio dump. The new one states the offer, shows recent work, and makes it easy to write to us. That is enough.

The mark is a small construction, not a decoration. It works in the tab, on a invoice, and on the door. Colour is one signal blue and a paper ground.

This refresh also cleaned the CMS so editors can add a project without breaking the grid. A studio site that only a developer can update is not a studio site.
