Northline makes considered lighting. Their old identity looked like a generic interiors blog. We rebuilt the name as a tight wordmark, set a paper-and-graphite palette, and wrote rules for how product photography should fall off the edge of the frame.

The system covers the site, sample boxes, a dealer binder, and a trade-fair wall. Every application uses the same crop logic so a lamp is always the subject, never a lifestyle extra.

We also cut the product catalogue from forty pages to sixteen. Dealers now send the PDF. That was the brief hiding under the brief: make the brand easier to sell.
