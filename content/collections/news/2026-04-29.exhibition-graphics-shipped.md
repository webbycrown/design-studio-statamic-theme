We designed the graphics for a photography exhibition that runs through May. The brief asked for quiet so the images could lead. We used one typeface, two weights, and a floor vinyl system that could be remade if a wall moved.

Entrance type is large and a little slower than usual. People arrive talking. They need a second to land. Small captions come later, next to the work.

The same files became the printed guide. One system, two scales. That is the kind of economy we look for on cultural jobs.
