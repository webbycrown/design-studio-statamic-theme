We are opening the studio on the last Friday of each month from four to six. There is coffee and a table of current boards. There is not a slide show.

Ask about process. Ask why a mark died. We would rather talk about decisions than about software.

Book a slot through the contact form so we know how many chairs to put out. Walk-ins are fine if the room still has space.
