Monday crits are short. Each designer brings one problem, not a tour. This week the nav was trying to be a story. We made it a list again.

The grid on a magazine site stayed even when the images were messy. That stubbornness is useful. Editorial work needs a floor the pictures can push against.

We also asked a client-facing page to show the price. Mystery is not premium when someone is ready to buy. Clarity is.
