For a book of studio photographs we sat with the printer before we set the text. They told us which black would hold on uncoated stock and which varnish would scuff in the post.

Those limits changed the layout. We gave images more margin and dropped a thin rule that would have vanished. The book looks calmer because the process was honest.

Digital-only jobs skip this step. Everything else should treat production as a collaborator, not a last-minute vendor.
