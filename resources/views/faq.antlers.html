faq's

How do we start a project with the studio?

Send a short brief, a timeline, and the first object you need — a site, a pack, or a mark. We reply with whether the timing fits and what a first week would look like.

How long does a brand system usually take?

A focused identity is often four to six weeks including a workshop. A site or packaging programme sits on top of that once the kit is signed.

Do you work with teams outside the city?

Yes. Workshops can be remote. Print reviews still happen with physical proofs. We will tell you when a job needs us in the room.

What do we receive at handoff?

Masters, exports, font licences, photo credits, a do-not list, and a named owner on both sides. The kit should be usable without calling us for every seasonal ad.

Can you join an existing brand instead of starting over?

Often. We audit what should stay, redraw what fails at small sizes, and write the missing rules. A refresh is a system problem, not a new logo by default.

How are fees structured?

Monthly studio retainers and fixed project fees. Both include a written scope. We do not start drawing against an open tab.
