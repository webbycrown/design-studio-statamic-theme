Graphic design in this studio means a finished object: a poster you can hang, a pack you can hold, a report someone might finish. We start from the constraint — paper, press, shelf, or screen — and draw until the thing works at the distance it will be seen.

You get a small set of applications, not a folder of leftovers. If a layout cannot survive the printer or the aisle, we do not present it.
