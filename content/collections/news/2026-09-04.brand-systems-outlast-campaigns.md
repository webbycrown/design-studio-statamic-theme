Campaigns get the applause. Systems keep the brand honest after the launch week. We now brief every identity with a living kit: type pairings, colour roles, photography rules, and a short do-not list.

The kit is small on purpose. Twelve slides beat a 90-page PDF that nobody opens. We show how a logo sits on a dark poster, a packing label, and a mobile header. If it fails in one of those places, we redraw before we present.

Last quarter two clients came back for seasonal work and used the kit without a kickoff. That is the test. A system should make the next job faster, not start another discovery call.
