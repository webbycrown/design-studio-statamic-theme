The previous report was a slide deck stapled into a PDF. We designed a document with a reading line, clear charts, and a summary that fits on two pages.

Photography is from the year, credited, and not stock. Numbers keep their source on the same spread.

Print and web share files. The web version is the same document, not a microsite that dies in twelve months.
