East Quay asked for signs that work in rain and in a hurry. We designed a three-level family with high-contrast type and arrows that do not rely on colour alone.

Route colours exist, but every sign also says the destination in words. Icons are backups, not the language.

The pilot set is live on the south path. If summer counts hold, the rest of the quay follows with the same drawings and a local fabricator we already briefed.
