We designed a season frame: a shared date lockup and a photography crop. Each play gets one image and a colour bar. Posters, tickets, and the site use the same frame.

Type is theatrical without being costume. It has to work on a rainy bus shelter.

Box office reported fewer 'which show is this' emails after the first month. That is a design result.
