Summer was a production season. We launched a restaurant group, a lighting brand, a clinic, a publisher, a product studio, and a civic festival. The work looked different. The bottlenecks did not.

Copy arrived late on four of the six. We now ask for final headlines before we build the hero. Placeholders make everyone optimistic and then the layout breaks when the real sentence is twice as long.

The festival site was the exception. They wrote first. Design took nine days instead of four weeks. We are putting that sequence into every kickoff from here.
