Designers in this studio export with a budget. Two type families. Images that match their display size. No autoplaying video above the fold unless the client has a reason that survives a conversation.

We measure the first contentful paint on a mid-range phone, not on the office fibre. That number changes what we allow in the hero.

One rebuild cut the homepage from 7MB to under 1MB and kept the same art direction. The difference was crop, format, and fewer decorative layers. Visitors felt it immediately.
