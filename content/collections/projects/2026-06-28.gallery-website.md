The gallery wanted less chrome and more picture. We designed a sequence that opens on the current show, then archives, then visit information. No carousel. No autoplay.

Captions sit under the image, not in a hover that desktop users forget and phone users never find. The type is small and even so it never competes with a print.

A collector mailing list is the only interrupt, and it waits until the second page. The first job of the site is to show the work properly.
