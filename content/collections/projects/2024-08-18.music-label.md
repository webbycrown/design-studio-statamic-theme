Low Gate releases one record a month. We designed a spine system, a centre-label template, and a site that is a list, not a mood video.

Each sleeve can be wild. The catalogue number and the spine stay obedient. Collectors can find things. That is the brand.

Merch uses the same stamp. We resisted a secondary logo for 'streetwear'. One mark is enough if it is drawn well.
