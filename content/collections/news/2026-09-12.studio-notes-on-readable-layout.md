We spent the last month reviewing twenty live sites against one question: can a new visitor understand the offer before the first scroll ends? Most of the work was not new colour. It was line length, heading hierarchy, and the space around the primary action.

Readable layout is a studio habit, not a trend. We set type sizes from the content, not from a template. We keep paragraphs short enough to scan, and we give each section one job. When a page tries to sell, explain, and prove everything at once, people leave.

This week we shipped a homepage for a product studio that had three competing headlines. We kept one sentence, moved proof into a later band, and the bounce rate dropped on the first day. That is the kind of news we like to share: quieter pages that work harder.
