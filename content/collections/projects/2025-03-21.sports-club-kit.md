The club needed a mark that could be vinyl-cut and embroidered. We drew a simple oar lock-up and a wordmark that survives cheap print runs for race day flyers.

Colour is one navy and a signal orange for safety kit. Merchandise uses the navy. Water uses both.

Members voted on two finals. The winner was the less clever one. Sporting identities should be shouted across a river, not decoded.
