A tea brand came in with a gold foil that vanished under supermarket LEDs. We rebuilt the contrast, kept the foil as a small hit, and the pack started to read from two metres away.

Packaging is graphic design with physics. Glue lines, barcode zones, and a hand that has three seconds to choose. We prototype on the real dieline, not on a mockup of a perfect cube.

The studio now keeps a shelf of printed tests. Clients can hold last week's version next to today's. That conversation is faster than another slide of alternatives.
