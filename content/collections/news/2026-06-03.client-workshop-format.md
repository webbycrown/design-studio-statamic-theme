Our old workshops were friendly and inconclusive. The new format is four hours: language, visual range, one shortlist, and a written decision. Someone in the room has to pick.

We bring three real directions, not twenty thumbnails. Each direction is shown on the same applications so taste is not confused with completeness.

Clients tell us the day feels intense. That is the point. A calm week of email after a vague workshop is more expensive than a hard afternoon.
