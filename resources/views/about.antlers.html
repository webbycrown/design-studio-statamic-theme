About our Studio

Design Studio is an independent practice for brands that need to be seen clearly. We draw marks, design sites, and art-direct objects for teams who want a system they can keep using.

The studio is small on purpose. You work with the people who present the work. We write in plain language and we will say no when a brief is only decoration.

We start every job with a one-page brief: who it is for, what must never change, and where the brand has to live next year. Drawing comes after that conversation, not before.

We shape identities that hold on a packed shelf and on a quiet homepage, then we leave a kit the next designer can use without calling us back for every seasonal ad.
