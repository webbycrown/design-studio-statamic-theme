A founder asked us to make their site feel premium. The first pass they expected more animation. We took things away. The logo sat larger. The type sat darker. The project grid had room to breathe.

Quiet is not empty. It is control. We keep one accent colour and let photography carry the energy. Clients who sell considered products rarely need a carnival at the top of the page.

After launch, their sales team started sending the homepage instead of a PDF. That is the brief succeeding: the site finally matches how they talk in the room.
