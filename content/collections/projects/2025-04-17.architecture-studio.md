Architects often bury projects under a manifesto. We reversed it. Each building gets a short fact set: year, place, client, and a paragraph. Drawings appear after photographs.

The office page is a single photograph of the room they actually work in. No render of a future HQ.

The CMS lets them add a project in the same template. The site will still look like itself in five years if they stay disciplined. We wrote that warning into the handbook.
